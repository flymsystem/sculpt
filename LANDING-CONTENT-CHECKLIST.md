## 4. Photographs — **needed before launch**

The site currently uses **stock gym photos from a design template**. They are placeholders and are not licensed for your use long-term.

| # | Detail | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | **1 hero photo** | The big one at the top. Wide/landscape, your best-looking angle of the floor. Shot in colour |
| 11 | **1 gym interior photo** | For the About section |
| 12 | **4 training photos** | One each for Strength & Conditioning, Personal Training, Group Classes, Cardio |

**Practical notes:**
- Phone photos are fine if taken in good light. Landscape, not portrait.
- Higher resolution is better — we can crop down, we cannot add detail.
- Interior shots are automatically styled black-and-blue on the site, so mismatched lighting between photos matters less than you would think.
- Avoid photos with other gyms' branding, posters or logos visible.
- If any member's face is clearly identifiable, get their okay first.
