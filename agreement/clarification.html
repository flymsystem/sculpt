04

Domain & Domain Renewal

This section is worth reading carefully — a domain is often confused with the website or the software itself.

In plain terms

A domain is simply the internet address or URL used to access a website — for example, dsculptfitness.com. The domain is not the website itself, the gym management software, the database, or the hosting/server.

Domain
The website address / URL people type or tap to reach the app.
Website / Software
The actual gym management application and its features.
Hosting
The infrastructure where the application runs and stores data.

Please note

The renewal price is not guaranteed to always be ₹800 — registrar pricing can change over time. The actual renewal amount will depend on the registrar's applicable pricing at the time of renewal. Domain renewal is a third-party recurring registration cost — it is not a development charge, not a charge for the website/software, and not a hosting charge. It is simply the recurring cost required to keep the chosen domain name registered.

Before the August 2027 renewal, Flym Technologies will inform D Sculpt Fitness of the applicable renewal amount and obtain confirmation/payment before proceeding.

Support, Future Work & Handover

05

Bug Fix & Ongoing Support

Included, at no extra cost

Flym Technologies will provide ongoing support for genuine bugs or errors affecting the agreed functionality of the delivered system, without additional development charges.

A bug is an issue where an existing agreed feature or functionality does not work as intended — for example: QR check-in failing due to an implementation issue, an existing invoice calculation behaving incorrectly, existing member functionality stopping working, or existing agreed functionality breaking.

06

Future Features & Improvements

Features and improvements that can reasonably be developed within the existing Flym platform will be provided without additional development charges, subject to technical feasibility and mutual discussion.

07

Third-Party Services & External Costs

Flym's development and support work is separate from third-party recurring costs that sit outside our control — for example, domain registration/renewal, paid third-party APIs, payment gateway charges, WhatsApp/communication provider charges, or other external subscriptions and services. If any third-party service requires a payment, Flym will communicate the applicable cost to D Sculpt Fitness before the cost is incurred, wherever reasonably possible.

08

Additional Work & Scope Clarity

To keep things simple, work falls into one of four categories:

Existing bugs — covered under ongoing support, no charge.
Reasonable improvements/features within the Flym platform — no development charge, subject to feasibility.
Third-party costs — passed through transparently, communicated in advance.
Work requiring fundamentally new systems, external products, hardware or infrastructure — scoped and quoted separately, only after discussion and approval.
